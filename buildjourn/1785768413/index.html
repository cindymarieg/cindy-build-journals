What this does

Every time you finish documenting a project — what was built, how it works, where things stand — this system takes that write-up and turns it into a real, permanent, shareable web page automatically. No copying files, no manual uploads, no separate publishing step.

It also keeps a running, searchable list of every one of these pages ever created, so nothing gets lost or forgotten.

Who it's for

Anyone documenting the systems, automations, or projects they build — and anyone who needs to come back later and remember exactly how something works, without digging through old notes or conversations.

What it automates

Once you finish writing up a project, everything after that — saving it, publishing it as a web page, and adding it to the searchable list — happens on its own within about a minute.

Prerequisites

Nothing to set up on your end — the publishing system itself is already built and running. You just need to know how to trigger it (see Daily Use).

First-run expectations

The first time you use this, expect the whole process — from finishing your write-up to seeing the live link — to take under two minutes. After that, it's the same every time.

How to trigger it

1
When you've finished documenting a project, just say so — phrases like "save the build journal," "document what we built," or "journal this" all work.
2
You'll be asked three quick things: the project's name, whether the work is complete or still in progress, and whether you also want a client-friendly version of the document.
3
You'll see a short list of everything it found to include — check it over and flag anything missing or wrong before it moves on.
4
You'll get a preview of the finished document to look over.
5
Once you confirm, it publishes on its own. No further steps.

What to expect

Within about 30–60 seconds of confirming, a live web page exists at its own permanent link — safe to bookmark, share, or come back to anytime. It also shows up automatically on the master list of every document like it.

Where outputs land

Every published document lives at its own unique link, and all of them are browsable and searchable from one home page. Ask whoever set this up for you if you don't have that home page link handy.

Common issues and fixes

Nothing published, and you don't know why: This system will proactively notify the team if something breaks — so if you don't see a link appear after a couple of minutes and you haven't heard anything, check in with whoever maintains this system.
Need to make a correction after publishing: Documents aren't edited in place — a corrected version gets published as a new entry, so the history of what changed and when stays intact.
